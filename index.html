Lagos, Nigeria

Lagos, Nigeria, is Africa’s most populous city and a powerhouse of culture, commerce, and creativity. Located on the Atlantic coast, this bustling megacity serves as the economic heart of the country, driving major industries in finance, tech, and entertainment—most notably Nollywood and the global Afrobeats music scene. Known for its relentless energy, vibrant street life, and iconic yellow "danfo" buses, Lagos seamlessly blends a rapid-fire urban hustle with stunning coastal landscapes like Victoria Island and Lekki. It is a city of sharp contrasts, deep cultural heritage, and an undeniable, infectious rhythm that captivates anyone who visits.
